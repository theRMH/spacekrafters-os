SpaceKrafters internal operating system. It combines the existing
operations application, the newly developed finance tracker, and the
requirements confirmed during discovery. Where an existing
implementation already exists, this PRD preserves it unless an
integration change is explicitly specified.

# 1. Product Vision

SpaceKrafters OS shall be the single operating system for
lead-to-handover interior execution, connecting CRM, site visits,
requirements, commercial documents, design, project execution,
procurement, company expenses, receivables, payables, cashflow and
management reporting. The Owner shall additionally retain a private
Personal Finance workspace and an Owner-only Combined Financial View.

## 1.1 Product Principles

-   One operational source of truth. Data created in a workflow must not
    be re-entered in another module.

-   Money movement is recorded once. Allocations and references do not
    create duplicate cash transactions.

-   Personal Finance and Company Finance are logically isolated.

-   Combined Financial View is read-only aggregation for the Owner.

-   Requested, approved, committed/due and paid are distinct financial
    states.

-   Client quotation value and internal project cost budget are separate
    concepts.

-   All material business changes are auditable.

-   Permissions are enforced at the data/server layer, not only by
    hiding UI.

-   Existing approved UI and working functionality should be extended
    surgically rather than rebuilt without need.

    client creation.

# 12. Finance Architecture

## 12.1 Two Financial Domains

| Domain \| Purpose \| Access \| Source of data \|

| --- \| --- \| --- \| --- \|

| Personal Finance \| Owner household/personal money, investments,
  insurance, utilities, subscriptions, personal plans. \| Owner only \|
  Existing Personal Finance Tracker, converted to personal-only usage.
  \|

| Company Finance \| SpaceKrafters operational cashflow, expenses,
  receivables, payables, project financials and overheads. \| Owner +
  Accounts; limited role views \| Company workflows and Accounts direct
  entries. \|

| Combined Financial View \| Owner management aggregation across both
  domains. \| Owner only \| Read-only aggregation. No ledger of its own.
  \|

## 12.2 Non-negotiable Finance Rules

-   No Personal/Office selector should be used to mix the two domains in
    the final UX. Domain is determined by where the record is created.

-   Company transactions generated by team workflows must not be
    manually re-entered by Accounts.

-   One cash movement remains one transaction even if allocated across
    projects/categories.

-   Approved commitment does not equal paid cash movement.

-   Combined View cannot create, edit or delete transactions.

-   Owner-to-company movements must use explicit
    transfer/funding/reimbursement treatment so they are not
    accidentally counted as normal revenue/expense twice.

| --- \| --- \| --- \|

# 15. Company Expense Lifecycle

| Canonical lifecycle`<br>`{=html}Requested -\> Approved -\> Due /
  Commitment -\> Paid -\> Closed. Rejected remains in history. Approval

| --- \|

## 15.1 Expense Request

-   Requestor, project if applicable, category/subcategory, purpose,
    amount, vendor/payee if known, needed-by date, attachments and
    remarks.

-   Owner-configurable permission to raise project expense requests.

-   Project expense must link to project; overhead need not.

## 15.2 Approval

-   Simple approval flow for initial implementation.

-   Owner or authorised approver can approve/reject.

-   Rejected requests remain permanently visible with reason, approver,
    timestamp and original values.

-   No complex amount-threshold multi-level approval engine in initial
    scope.

## 15.3 Commitment / Due

-   Approved unpaid amount appears as upcoming commitment/payable.

-   Commitments influence forecast/upcoming cash requirements but not
    actual cashflow.

## 15.4 Payment

-   Accounts records actual amount paid, date, account, payment mode,
    UTR/reference, invoice/bill and proof.

-   Partial payments supported.

-   Only paid amount becomes actual cash movement.

-   The same business expense record continues through the lifecycle;
    linked payment records may exist technically, but no disconnected
    duplicate expense is created.

## 15.5 Company Expense Reporting

-   Project Expenses and Company Overheads are separately reportable.

-   Both roll up to Total Company Expenses and Company Cashflow.

payment can allocate ₹500 to Project A, ₹400 to Project B and ₹100 to
Project C while remaining one ₹1,000 cash movement.

# 28. Acceptance Criteria & Critical Edge Cases

| Acceptance area \| Required outcome \|

| --- \| --- \|

| No duplicate company expense \| A project/procurement expense is
  entered once and progresses through request, approval, payable and
  payment without Accounts re-entering the expense. \|

| Commitment vs cash \| Approved unpaid expense appears as
  commitment/payable but does not reduce actual cash until payment. \|

| Rejected request history \| Rejected expense remains
  searchable/auditable with reason and does not affect cashflow. \|

| Multi-project payment \| One ₹1,000 payment allocated across projects
  remains one bank transaction. \|

| Employee/owner reimbursement \| Underlying expense is recorded once;
  reimbursement settlement does not create a second expense. \|

| Client advance \| Advance is not counted again as new revenue when
  later allocated. \|

| Statement duplicate \| Importing the same statement twice does not
  duplicate confirmed transactions. \|

| Quotation integration \| Accepted quotation creates/updates project
  commercial values, milestones and projections without duplicate manual
  entry. \|

| Budget separation \| Client quotation and internal cost budget remain
  separate. \|

| Late task \| Late update is allowed, marked Late, requires reason and
  escalates. \|

| Personal privacy \| Accounts/team users cannot query or access
  Personal Finance data. \|

| Combined view \| Combined View can aggregate and drill down but cannot
  create/edit/delete source transactions. \|

| Historical Office migration \| Old Office records are
  migrated/reviewed without silent loss or duplication. \|

| FY closure \| Closed FY blocks normal edits; Owner reopen requires
  reason and audit. \|

| Audit \| Material changes show who changed what and when. \|

# 31. Final Product Success Criteria

-   SpaceKrafters can operate lead-to-project-closure in one connected
    company system.

-   Team-generated company expenses flow automatically into Company
    Finance without duplicate Accounts entry.

-   Owner can distinguish commitments, payables and actual cash
    movement.

-   Every project can show contract value, internal budget, actual cost,
    collections, outstanding amounts and margin.

-   Accounts can trace each company financial amount to account, party,
    project and originating workflow.

-   Purchase can trace requirement through quotation/PO/material/vendor
    bill/payment.

-   Team accountability is visible through tasks, Gantt, deadlines, late
    reasons, activities and audit history.

-   Personal Finance remains private and retains the functionality of
    the current developed tracker.

-   Owner can see a Combined Financial View without merging or
    compromising the Personal and Company ledgers.

-   Historical data, imports and financial-year records remain auditable
    and non-duplicative.
